# UMTUBA_LEARNING_PARTNER_MARKETPLACE_FOUNDATION_V1

## Status

FOUNDATION_READY — isolated sandbox + safety gates. Affiliate applications not submitted.

## Goal

Begin UMTUBA Learning as a multi-provider discovery marketplace (Coursera, edX, Skillshare, Udemy): Arabic/English browse, taxonomy, search, comparison, certificate transparency, ranking, saved courses, learning-path foundation, and provider-agnostic affiliate records.

## Legal / product model

- UMTUBA does not claim third-party courses as UMTUBA-created.
- Every external row keeps original title, institution/instructor, source platform, official URL, certificate metadata, pricing model, and attribution.
- No copy, download, rehost, scrape, or mirror of course video/content.
- No fabricated live affiliate links or course facts.
- No automatic affiliate applications.
- UMTUBA does not issue third-party certificates.
- “Accredited” / “officially recognized” appears only when verified for that specific program and issuer.

## Pilot honesty

Automated provider feeds are unavailable before affiliate approval. The local file is a **23-course factual QA sample** of well-known official public landing URLs. Ratings and exact prices were not invented. Skillshare has **0** individual class rows. Teacher Development and Marketing have no sample rows. Do not treat this as a live catalog of 100.
