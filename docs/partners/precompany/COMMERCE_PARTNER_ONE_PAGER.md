# Commerce partner one-pager (internal)

Purpose: explain how a future Store partner would be reviewed. Not an offer letter.

## What UMTUBA would need later

1. A written contract and an approved legal review.
2. An explicit rights matrix: catalog display, images, price sync, inventory sync, checkout, resell.
3. A credential stored only as a vault reference. Never plaintext in tickets or git.
4. A data class. REAL_PARTNER_DATA cannot be ACTIVE in the current foundation.
5. Category metadata that can map to UMTUBA’s normalized taxonomy (Electronics, Fashion, Home, Beauty, Sports, Books, Accessories, Kids, Automotive Accessories, Office, Digital/Other).

## What UMTUBA will not do now

- Import unauthorized third-party catalogs (including SHEIN, Temu, Amazon, AliExpress, Alibaba, Trendyol, eBay, DHgate, and similar).
- Mark a real partnership ACTIVE.
- Send outbound partnership mail from this pack.
- Turn DEMO or MOCK items into production-purchasable SKUs.
