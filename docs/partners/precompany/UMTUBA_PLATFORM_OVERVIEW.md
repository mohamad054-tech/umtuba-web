# UMTUBA platform overview (internal)

Audience: future partner conversations. Not a public launch claim.

## What UMTUBA is

UMTUBA is a multi-product platform. Members use a shared account across Home/Watch, Messages, Store, Learning, and World. Store and Learning are being developed before company registration is complete. That decision does not make any real partnership ACTIVE.

## What is live enough to discuss later

- Store catalog, search, filters, product detail, variants, favorites, cart, and checkout-mode routing exist as product surfaces.
- Learning has courses, lessons, progress, quizzes, and an UMTUBA-owned certificate path for UMTUBA Originals.
- A provider-neutral import and rights layer exists so a later approved partner can be reviewed without inventing a second stack.

## What is not claimed

- No live unauthorized marketplace inventory.
- No outbound partnership program is running.
- No UMTUBA certificate is an accredited university or government credential.
- No real payouts or tax collection are implemented. Ledger rows in the foundation are placeholders.
