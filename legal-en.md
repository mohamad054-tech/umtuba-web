**Last updated:** 13 September 2026
**Effective:** on public launch

## Who we are

UMTUBA Limited (in registration, Republic of Ireland) operates umtuba.com and the

- Registered office: `[[REGISTERED ADDRESS]]`

## What we collect

**You give us directly:**
- Account data: username, display name, email address, password (stored hashed, never in readable form)
- Profile data: photo, cover image, biography, website link, and optionally city and country
- Content: videos, images, text posts, comments, live streams, messages
- Communications: anything you send to our support addresses

**Collected automatically:**
- Technical data: IP address, browser type, device type, operating system, language
- Usage data: pages viewed, videos watched, interactions, session timing
- Cookies and similar technologies (see our Cookie Policy)

**From third parties:**
- Payment status from our payment processors (we never receive your full card number)
- App install and crash data from Google Play and the Apple App Store

## Why we use it, and our legal basis

| Purpose | Legal basis (GDPR Art. 6) |
|---|---|
| Providing the service you signed up for | Contract |
| Keeping the platform safe; preventing abuse and fraud | Legitimate interests |
| Responding to your support requests | Contract / Legitimate interests |
| Improving the product and fixing faults | Legitimate interests |
| Sending optional marketing messages | Consent |
| Non-essential cookies and analytics | Consent |
| Complying with legal obligations | Legal obligation |

You may withdraw consent at any time. Withdrawal does not affect processing carried
out before withdrawal.

## Location data

Location is optional. We do not track you continuously.

Profile city and country are **private by default**. They become visible to others
only if you choose to publish them in your privacy settings.

Location attached to a post is separate: you choose it when publishing, and it is
shown with that post.

## Who we share with

We do not sell your personal data.

We share with:
- **Service providers** who process data on our instructions: hosting, storage,
  content delivery, email delivery, payment processing, crash reporting
- **Other users**, where you have chosen to publish content or profile information
- **Authorities**, where we are legally required to disclose

All processors are bound by written agreements requiring confidentiality and
security measures equivalent to our own.

## International transfers

Some providers operate outside the European Economic Area. Where that happens, we
rely on the European Commission's Standard Contractual Clauses or an adequacy
decision. You may request a copy of the safeguards by writing to privacy@umtuba.com.

## How long we keep it

| Data | Retention |
|---|---|
| Account and profile | While your account is open |
| Content you publish | While published, or until you delete it |
| Content you delete | Up to 30 days in soft-deleted state, then permanently removed |
| Deleted accounts | Up to 30 days, then removed except where law requires longer |
| Server and security logs | Up to 12 months |
| Records of legal complaints and safety reports | Up to 24 months |
| Transaction and tax records | 6 years, as required by Irish law |

## Your rights

Under the GDPR you have the right to:
- **Access** the personal data we hold about you
- **Rectify** inaccurate or incomplete data
- **Erase** your data ("right to be forgotten")
- **Restrict** how we process your data
- **Portability** — receive your data in a machine-readable format
- **Object** to processing based on legitimate interests
- **Withdraw consent** at any time
- **Not be subject** to a solely automated decision with legal or similarly
  significant effects

To exercise any of these, use the request forms in your account settings, or email
privacy@umtuba.com. We respond within one month. That period may be extended by two
further months for complex requests; we will tell you if that happens.

If you are unhappy with our response, you may lodge a complaint with the Irish Data
Protection Commission (dataprotection.ie) or the supervisory authority in your country
of residence.

## Security

We use encryption in transit (HTTPS), access controls, database-level row and column
permissions, and regular review of who can reach production systems. No system is
completely secure; we will notify you and the relevant authority of a personal data
breach where the law requires it.

## Children

UMTUBA is not directed at children under 16. We do not knowingly collect personal
data from children under 16. If you believe a child has provided us with personal
data, write to privacy@umtuba.com and we will delete it.

## Changes

We will post any changes on this page and update the date above. For significant
changes we will notify you in the app or by email before they take effect.

# 4. Community Guidelines

**Last updated:** 13 September 2026

UMTUBA exists so ideas can cross borders. That only works if people are safe here.

## Not allowed

**Content involving minors.** Nothing that sexualises, endangers or exploits anyone
under 18. No exceptions, no context that makes it acceptable. We remove it
immediately, permanently ban the account, and report it to the relevant authorities.

**Violence and dangerous behaviour.** No threats, no glorification of violence, no
content encouraging self-harm, eating disorders, or dangerous challenges.

**Hate.** No attacks on people based on race, ethnicity, national origin, religion,
disability, disease, gender, gender identity, sexual orientation, caste, or
immigration status.

**Harassment.** No targeted abuse, no doxxing, no sharing private images without
consent, no coordinated pile-ons.

**Adult content.** No pornography or sexually explicit material.

**Deception.** No impersonation, no scams, no manipulated media presented as real, no
coordinated inauthentic behaviour.

**Illegal goods.** No sale of drugs, weapons, counterfeit goods, stolen data, or
regulated items without proper authorisation.

**Spam.** No bulk unsolicited messaging, no engagement farming, no artificial
amplification.

**Intellectual property.** Do not post content you do not have the right to post.

## What happens when you break these rules

Depending on severity and history:

| Action | When |
|---|---|
| Content removed | First or minor breach |
| Reduced distribution | Repeated borderline content |
| Temporary suspension | Repeated or serious breach |
| Permanent ban | Severe breach, or repeated suspensions |

Content involving minors, credible threats of violence, and coordinated harm go
straight to permanent ban.

## Reporting

Use the report button on any content or profile, or write to legal@umtuba.com. We
review every report. Reporting is confidential — the person you report is not told who
reported them.

## Appeals

If you believe we made a mistake, write to legal@umtuba.com within 30 days. Tell us
your username and what was removed. A different reviewer will look at it.

# 5. Copyright and DMCA Policy

**Last updated:** 13 September 2026

UMTUBA Limited respects intellectual property and expects its users to do the same.

## Reporting infringement

Send a notice to **dmca@umtuba.com** containing:

1. Your physical or electronic signature
2. Identification of the copyrighted work you claim has been infringed
3. The URL or other specific location of the material on UMTUBA
4. Your name, address, telephone number and email address
5. A statement that you have a good-faith belief that the use is not authorised by the

6. A statement, under penalty of perjury, that the information in your notice is

Incomplete notices may be rejected.

## What we do

We review valid notices, remove or disable access to the material, and notify the
user who posted it, including a copy of your notice.

## Counter-notice

If your content was removed and you believe this was a mistake or misidentification,
send a counter-notice to dmca@umtuba.com containing:

1. Your signature
2. Identification of the removed material and where it appeared
3. A statement under penalty of perjury that you believe in good faith the material
   was removed as a result of mistake or misidentification
4. Your name, address and telephone number
5. Consent to the jurisdiction of the courts of Ireland, or of your district if you
   are in the United States

We may restore the material after 10 to 14 business days unless the original
complainant informs us they have started court proceedings.

## Repeat infringers

Accounts that repeatedly infringe are terminated.

## Misuse

Knowingly filing a false notice may make you liable for damages. Please be sure before
you file.

# 8. Delete Your Account

You can request deletion of your UMTUBA account at any time.

## What gets deleted

- Your profile, including name, biography, photos and links
- Your posts, videos, images and comments
- Your messages
- Your follow relationships
- Your activity history and preferences

## What we keep, and why

- **Transaction and tax records** — 6 years, required by Irish law
- **Safety and moderation records** — up to 24 months, where an account was actioned
  for a serious breach, to prevent the same person returning
- **Server logs** — up to 12 months, then automatically removed
- **Content others have shared** — copies already reposted by other users remain with
  those users

## How to request it

**If you are signed in:** Settings → Account → Delete account.

**If you cannot sign in:** email privacy@umtuba.com from the address registered to
the account, with the subject "Account deletion request". We will verify your
identity before acting.

## What happens next

1. We confirm your request by email
2. Your account is deactivated immediately and is no longer visible to others
3. There is a 14-day grace period during which you can cancel by signing in
4. After 14 days, deletion is permanent and cannot be reversed
5. We email you when deletion is complete

## Questions

privacy@umtuba.com

# 9. Export Your Data

You have the right to receive a copy of the personal data we hold about you, in a
structured, machine-readable format (GDPR Article 20).

## What the export includes

- Profile information
- Your posts, including captions and metadata
- Your comments
- Your messages
- Your follows and followers
- Your account settings and preferences
- Your activity history

Videos and images are included as files alongside a JSON index.

## How to request it

**If you are signed in:** Settings → Account → Export my data.

**If you cannot sign in:** email privacy@umtuba.com from the registered address with
the subject "Data export request".

## What happens next

1. We confirm your request
2. We prepare the export — this usually takes a few days and may take up to one month
3. We email you a secure download link
4. The link expires after 7 days

## Format

A ZIP archive containing JSON files and your media. JSON is readable by common tools
and can be imported elsewhere.
